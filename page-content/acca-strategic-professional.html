What is the ACCA Strategic Professional level?

Strategic Professional is the third and final level of the ACCA Qualification — the exams that separate students from members. You sit four papers: two compulsory Essentials — Strategic Business Leader (SBL) and Strategic Business Reporting (SBR) — plus two Options chosen from Advanced Financial Management (AFM), Advanced Performance Management (APM), Advanced Taxation (ATX) and Advanced Audit and Assurance (AAA).

All four exams are session-based, held in March, June, September and December. SBL is a four-hour case-study exam built around a single organisation; SBR and the options run three hours and fifteen minutes each. Every paper awards dedicated professional skills marks — for scepticism, analysis, commercial acumen and communication — which is why exam technique matters more here than anywhere else in ACCA. Note the seven-year rule: from your first Strategic Professional pass, you have seven years to complete the remaining exams.

How should I choose my ACCA options papers?

Pick the options that extend your strongest Applied Skills papers and match your career direction: AFM builds on FM and suits corporate finance and treasury; APM builds on PM for performance and consulting roles; ATX builds on TX for tax specialists; AAA builds on AA and is effectively essential for a career in audit. Choosing options you're genuinely strong in matters — pass rates at this level range from roughly 30% to 50%, the lowest in the qualification.

ACCA Strategic Professional FAQs.

What is the ACCA Strategic Professional level?
It's the final level of the ACCA Qualification: two compulsory essentials — Strategic Business Leader (SBL) and Strategic Business Reporting (SBR) — plus two options chosen from AFM, APM, ATX and AAA. Pass all four, complete the Ethics module and your practical experience, and you qualify for ACCA membership.
Which options papers should I choose?
Match your options to your strongest Applied Skills papers and your career: AFM builds on FM (corporate finance), APM on PM (performance and consulting), ATX on TX (tax practice), AAA on AA (audit — effectively essential for a practising certificate). Book a free meeting and we'll help you decide before you commit.
How hard are these exams?
The hardest in ACCA — global pass rates run between roughly 30% and 50%. They test application and professional judgement in realistic scenarios, with dedicated professional skills marks in every paper. Technique, practice and quality feedback are what move the needle, and our courses are built around all three.
What is the exam format?
SBL is a single four-hour case-study exam. SBR and the options papers are 3 hours 15 minutes each, mixing compulsory scenario questions with choice. All are session exams held in March, June, September and December, with a 50% pass mark.
Is there a time limit at this level?
Yes — the seven-year rule. From the date you pass your first Strategic Professional exam, you have seven years to pass the rest. Passes expire if you exceed the limit and must be retaken. The earlier levels have no time limit.
What else do I need to become an ACCA member?
Three things: pass the exams, complete the Ethics and Professional Skills Module (EPSM — best done before or alongside SBL, which it directly supports), and record 36 months of relevant practical experience (PER) with a workplace mentor. Then you apply for membership.
Should I do the Ethics module before SBL?
ACCA recommends it, and so do we. The EPSM teaches the professional skills — analysis, scepticism, communication — that SBL awards marks for directly. Students who complete EPSM first consistently perform better in SBL.
