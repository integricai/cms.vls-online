What is the CIMA Certificate in Business Accounting?

The CIMA Certificate in Business Accounting — Cert BA, often just called the CIMA Certificate Level — is the entry route into the CIMA qualification and the world's largest professional body of management accountants. It consists of four exams: BA1 Fundamentals of Business Economics, BA2 Fundamentals of Management Accounting, BA3 Fundamentals of Financial Accounting and BA4 Fundamentals of Ethics, Corporate Governance and Business Law.

There are no entry requirements — anyone can register, whatever their academic background. All four exams are on-demand, two-hour computer-based exams you can sit year-round at a test centre or online, in any order, with results available immediately. The Cert BA is a recognised qualification in its own right, and completing it takes you directly into the CIMA Professional Qualification at the Operational Level.

Who is the CIMA Certificate Level for?

It's designed for school leavers starting a business career, career changers moving into finance, and working professionals in accounts, banking or operations who want a globally recognised management accounting credential. If you hold a relevant business or accounting degree, you may be exempt from some or all four papers — we check your exemptions free of charge before you enrol.

Questions, answered

CIMA Certificate Level FAQs.

Do I need any qualifications to start the CIMA Certificate Level?
No — there are no formal entry requirements. The Certificate in Business Accounting is open to anyone, whatever your academic background. It's designed as the starting point for school leavers, career changers and anyone new to business and finance.
How many exams are there, and what's the format?
Four exams: BA1, BA2, BA3 and BA4. Each is a two-hour objective-test computer exam available on demand year-round — sit them at a test centre or online, in any order, with your result available immediately.
In what order should I take BA1–BA4?
Any order is allowed. Students new to accounting often start with BA3 (financial accounting basics) or BA2 (management accounting — the heart of CIMA), keeping BA4 as a lighter narrative paper to pair with a numerical one. We'll suggest an order for your background in a free consultation.
How long does the Certificate Level take?
Studying alongside work, most students complete all four exams in six to twelve months. Full-time students can finish in three to six. Because exams are on demand, you book each one only when you're ready.
Can I get exemptions?
Yes — a relevant business or accounting degree can exempt you from some or all four papers, letting you start directly at the Operational Level of the Professional Qualification. We check your exemptions free of charge before you enrol.
Is the Cert BA a qualification on its own?
Yes. The Certificate in Business Accounting is a recognised standalone qualification you can put on your CV — and it doubles as your entry ticket to the CIMA Professional Qualification and, ultimately, the CGMA designation.
What's the difference between CIMA and ACCA?
CIMA specialises in management accounting — business strategy, performance and decision support — while ACCA is broader, covering audit, tax and financial reporting in more depth. Both are globally recognised. If you're weighing them up, book a free meeting and we'll help you choose based on your career goals; VLS teaches both.
