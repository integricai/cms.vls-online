What is the ACCA Applied Skills level?

Applied Skills is the second level of the ACCA Professional Qualification — six exams that turn your foundation knowledge into the practical, technical skills of a working finance professional: Corporate and Business Law (LW), Performance Management (PM), Taxation (TX), Financial Reporting (FR), Audit and Assurance (AA) and Financial Management (FM).

Five of the six papers (PM, TX, FR, AA, FM) are session-based computer exams held four times a year — March, June, September and December — each lasting three hours with a 50% pass mark. LW is an on-demand CBE you can sit at any time, which is why many students clear it first. Complete Applied Knowledge and Applied Skills and you earn the ACCA Advanced Diploma in Accounting and Business — a respected credential in its own right, and your ticket to the final Strategic Professional level.

How difficult is Applied Skills?

This level is a real step up: global pass rates sit between roughly 40% and 55%, compared with 60–85% at Applied Knowledge. The difference between passing and failing is rarely knowledge — it's question practice and exam technique under time pressure. That's why every VLS Applied Skills course pairs full-syllabus video tuition with exam-standard question practice, a marked mock exam, and live revision sessions before each sitting.

Questions, answered

ACCA Applied Skills FAQs.

What is the ACCA Applied Skills level?
It's the second level of the ACCA Professional Qualification — six exams (LW, PM, TX, FR, AA, FM) that build the practical, technical skills of a professional accountant. Completing it alongside Applied Knowledge earns you the ACCA Advanced Diploma in Accounting and Business.
In what order should I take the six exams?
A common route: LW first (on-demand, clears quickly), then PM and TX, then FR, then AA (which builds on FR), and FM last. But the ideal order depends on your background and how many papers you can handle per session — book a free meeting and we'll plan it with you.
When are the exam sessions?
PM, TX, FR, AA and FM run at four sessions a year — March, June, September and December. You can attempt up to four papers per session, though one or two is realistic for most working students. LW is on-demand and can be sat at any time of year.
How hard are the Applied Skills exams?
Noticeably harder than Applied Knowledge — global pass rates run between roughly 40% and 55%. Each exam is 3 hours (LW is 2) with a 50% pass mark. The gap between passing and failing is usually exam technique and question practice, which is exactly what our courses drill.
How long does this level take?
Taking one to two papers per session, most working students complete all six exams in 12 to 24 months. There's no time limit on Applied Skills exams, so you can pace it around your life.
What qualification do I earn at the end?
Completing Applied Knowledge and Applied Skills earns you the ACCA Advanced Diploma in Accounting and Business — a respected credential on your CV — and unlocks the final Strategic Professional level on your route to full ACCA membership.
Do you cover the UK and international variants of TX and LW?
Our courses are built around the most widely sat variants. Contact us with the variant you're registered for and we'll confirm coverage and recommend the right course before you enrol.
