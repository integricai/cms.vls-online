What is the ACCA Foundation Diploma?

The ACCA Foundation Diploma — officially part of ACCA's Foundations in Accountancy (FIA) suite — is the recognised starting point for anyone who wants to become an ACCA-qualified accountant but doesn't yet hold the standard entry qualifications. The ACCA Professional Qualification normally requires two A-Levels and three GCSEs (or a degree). If you don't have those, the Foundation Diploma is your route in: complete it, and you continue straight into the ACCA Qualification with exemptions from the first three exams.

The Diploma consists of seven computer-based exams: FA1 (Recording Financial Transactions), MA1 (Management Information), FA2 (Maintaining Financial Records), MA2 (Managing Costs and Finance), FBT (Business and Technology), FMA (Management Accounting) and FFA (Financial Accounting). Exams are on-demand CBEs, so you can sit them at any licensed centre, at any time of the year — there are no fixed exam sittings holding you back.

Who is the ACCA Foundation Diploma for?

It's designed for school leavers with O-Levels, Matriculation or Intermediate qualifications, career changers moving into accounting and finance, and working bookkeepers or accounts assistants who want a globally recognised qualification. There are no formal entry requirements and no time limit — most Vertex students complete the full Diploma in twelve to fifteen months while working or studying.

Questions, answered

ACCA Foundation Diploma FAQs.

What are the entry requirements for the ACCA Foundation Diploma?
There are no formal entry requirements. If you have three O-Levels, Matriculation, Intermediate, or around ten years of school education, you can register. The Foundation Diploma is the recognised fast-track route into ACCA for students without A-Levels or a university degree.
How many papers are in the Foundation Diploma?
Seven: FA1 Recording Financial Transactions, MA1 Management Information, FA2 Maintaining Financial Records, MA2 Managing Costs and Finance, FBT Business and Technology, FMA Management Accounting, and FFA Financial Accounting. All are on-demand computer-based exams.
Do I get ACCA exemptions after completing the Foundation Diploma?
Yes. Once you complete all seven exams you're automatically transferred to the ACCA Qualification with exemptions from the three Applied Knowledge exams — BT, MA and FA — so you continue directly at the Applied Skills level.
How long does it take to complete?
There's no time limit, so you can fit study around work and life. Most students complete the Foundation Diploma in twelve to fifteen months. Because exams are on-demand CBEs, you can book each exam whenever you're ready, at any time of year.
How much does it cost?
ACCA charges a one-time registration fee (from £45, varying by country) and an annual subscription of £112. Exam fees are paid per paper to your local CBE centre. VLS tuition is $60–90 per paper, with everything included — and we can help arrange your ACCA registration and fee transfer.
Should I start with FA1/MA1 or go straight to FBT, FMA and FFA?
ACCA strongly recommends students without prior accounting knowledge start with FA1 and MA1, then FA2 and MA2, and finish with FBT, FMA and FFA. If you already have relevant study or work experience, book a free meeting and our tutors will advise the right entry point for you.
How do I register and book exams?
Register online at the ACCA website, selecting "Foundations in Accountancy" — applications take about ten days to process. When you're ready to sit an exam, find a licensed CBE centre near you and book directly. The VLS team can assist with the whole process, from application to exam booking.
