    ACCA AAA — Advanced Audit and Assurance Complete Study Notes

    ACCA AAA - Advanced Audit and Assurance takes your audit knowledge to the Strategic Professional level, covering advanced audit planning, professional ethics, risk assessment, group audits, the audit of specialised entities, and the evaluation of audit evidence in complex and challenging scenarios. Our Complete Notes run to approximately 230 pages, written by our expert academic team to substitute the lengthy study text while keeping you fully exam-focused.

    The notes are available as a downloadable PDF or as a searchable, zoomable online HD ebook with topic indexing, giving you flexibility to study however suits you best.
