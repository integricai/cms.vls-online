Vertex Learning Solutions (“Vertex”, “VLS”, “we”, “us” or “our”) is committed to protecting your privacy. This policy explains what personal data we collect through vls-online.com and our learning platform, why we collect it, how we use it, and the rights you have over it under the UK GDPR and the Data Protection Act 2018.

The short version
  • We only collect the data we need to deliver your courses and tutor support.
  • We never sell your personal data to anyone.
  • You can access, correct or delete your data at any time.
01

Who we are

Vertex Learning Solutions is the data controller responsible for your personal data. We provide online exam-preparation courses for ACCA, CIMA, CMA (USA) and CIA qualifications. If you have any questions about this policy or how we handle your information, you can reach our data protection contact using the details at the end of this page.

We are registered with the UK Information Commissioner's Office (ICO) and process personal data in line with UK data protection law.

02

Information we collect

We collect personal data that you give us directly, data generated as you use our platform, and a limited amount of technical data collected automatically.

CategoryWhat it includes
Account detailsYour name, email address, country and the qualification you're studying.
Payment dataBilling details processed securely by our payment provider — we don't store full card numbers.
Learning activityCourses enrolled, lesson progress, quiz and mock-exam results.
Support messagesQuestions and messages you send us, including via your WhatsApp study group.
Technical dataDevice type, browser, and anonymised usage data via cookies (see section 9).
03

How we use your data

We use your personal data to:

  • Create and manage your account and give you access to your courses.
  • Provide tutor support, weekly live classes and feedback on your progress.
  • Process payments and send you the receipts and confirmations you need.
  • Respond to your enquiries and provide customer support.
  • Improve our courses, platform and learning experience.
  • Send you service updates, and — only with your consent — marketing about new courses.
04

Legal bases for processing

We rely on different legal bases depending on the purpose: performance of a contract to deliver the courses you enrol in; consent for optional marketing, which you can withdraw at any time; legitimate interests to improve and secure our services; and legal obligation where we're required to keep records, for example for tax.

05

Sharing your data

We share data only with trusted service providers who help us run our platform — including payment processors, email delivery, secure hosting and messaging services — and only to the extent they need it to perform their service. We require every provider to protect your data and use it solely on our instructions. We never sell your personal data.

06

International transfers

As our students and providers are based around the world, your data may be processed outside the UK or EEA. Where it is, we put appropriate safeguards in place — such as the UK International Data Transfer Agreement or Standard Contractual Clauses — so your data receives an equivalent level of protection.

07

How long we keep it

We keep your personal data only for as long as needed for the purposes set out in this policy. Account and learning data are retained while your account is active and for a reasonable period afterwards; some records are kept longer where the law requires it. When data is no longer needed, we securely delete or anonymise it.

08

Your rights

Under UK data protection law you have a number of rights over your personal data. You can exercise any of these by contacting us.

Your rights at a glance

Access the data we hold about you
Correct inaccurate information
Request erasure of your data
Restrict or object to processing
Receive a portable copy of your data
Withdraw consent at any time

You also have the right to lodge a complaint with the ICO if you believe we have not handled your data properly — though we'd always appreciate the chance to put things right first.

09

Cookies & tracking

We use a small number of cookies to keep you signed in, remember your preferences and understand how the platform is used. You can manage your preferences at any time. For full details of every cookie we set, please see our Cookie Policy.

10

Data security

We use appropriate technical and organisational measures to protect your data, including encryption in transit, access controls and secure, monitored hosting. While no online service can be completely risk-free, we work hard to keep your information safe and review our measures regularly.

11

Children's privacy

Our courses are designed for working professionals and students pursuing professional qualifications. They are not directed at children, and we do not knowingly collect data from anyone under the age at which they can lawfully consent. If you believe a child has provided us with data, please contact us and we will remove it.

12

Changes & contact

We may update this policy from time to time. When we make material changes, we'll update the date at the top of the page and, where appropriate, let you know directly. Please check back occasionally so you're aware of how we're protecting your data.
