What's inside

What do the ACCA AAA notes cover?

The notes follow the current ACCA Advanced Audit and Assurance syllabus and study guide, so every examinable area is covered in an exam-focused format — including the International Standards on Auditing (ISAs) as they are tested at Strategic Professional level.

ARegulatory environment & professional and ethical considerations
The IESBA Code, threats and safeguards, NOCLAR, money laundering, and the auditor's legal and regulatory responsibilities.
BPractice management & quality management
Quality management at firm and engagement level (ISQM 1), tendering, fees, and professional liability of auditors.
CPlanning & conducting an audit of historical financial information
Audit risk assessment, materiality, group audits under ISA 600, using the work of others, and evidence in complex scenarios.
DCompletion, review & reporting
Evaluation of misstatements, going concern, subsequent events, auditor's reports, opinions and modifications.
EOther assignments & specialised engagements
Audit of specialised entities, prospective financial information, due diligence, forensic audit and assurance engagements.
FCurrent issues & developments
Data analytics, professional scepticism, audit quality and other current developments regularly tested in the AAA exam.
