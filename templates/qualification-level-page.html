What is the ACCA Applied Knowledge level?

Applied Knowledge is the first level of the ACCA Professional Qualification. It introduces the world of business and finance through three exams: Business and Technology (BT), Management Accounting (MA) and Financial Accounting (FA). Together they give you the essential understanding of how organisations work, how costs are managed, and how financial statements are prepared — the foundation everything else in ACCA is built on.

All three are on-demand computer-based exams (CBEs): two hours each, objective-test format, 50% pass mark, and bookable at any licensed exam centre at any time of the year. You can sit them in any order and at your own pace — there are no fixed exam sittings at this level. Completing Applied Knowledge and Applied Skills earns you the ACCA Advanced Diploma in Accounting and Business.

Who studies at this level?

Applied Knowledge is where you begin if you're entering ACCA with A-Levels or a non-relevant degree, or after completing the ACCA Foundation Diploma (in which case you may already be exempt — Foundation Diploma graduates skip BT, MA and FA entirely). Holders of relevant accounting degrees may also claim exemptions from some or all three exams; we check your exemptions free of charge before you enrol, so you never pay for a course you don't need.

Questions, answered

ACCA Applied Knowledge FAQs.

What is the ACCA Applied Knowledge level?
It's the first level of the ACCA Professional Qualification, made up of three exams — BT (Business and Technology), MA (Management Accounting) and FA (Financial Accounting). It builds the broad business and accounting foundation the rest of the qualification depends on.
In what order should I take BT, MA and FA?
Any order you like. Most students start with BT because it's fully narrative with no calculations, then take MA and FA. If you have a bookkeeping background, starting with FA can build early momentum. Book a free meeting and we'll plan the ideal order for your situation.
How hard are the Applied Knowledge exams?
They're the most accessible ACCA exams, with global pass rates typically between 60% and 85%. Each is a 2-hour objective-test CBE with a 50% pass mark. With structured tuition and plenty of question practice, most VLS students pass first time.
Can I get exemptions from these exams?
Possibly. Relevant accounting degrees, the ACCA Foundation Diploma and certain other qualifications can exempt you from some or all of BT, MA and FA. We check your exemptions free of charge before you enrol, so you never buy a course you don't need.
How long does this level take to complete?
Because exams are on-demand, you set the pace. Studying one paper at a time alongside work, most students complete all three in six to nine months; full-time students can finish in three to four. There's no deadline pressure — book each exam only when you're ready.
How do I book an Applied Knowledge exam?
Once registered as an ACCA student, find a licensed CBE centre near you and book directly with them — exams run year-round. Results are available immediately after the exam. The VLS team can help with registration and booking if you're unsure of the process.
What do I study after Applied Knowledge?
You progress to the Applied Skills level — six exams (LW, PM, TX, FR, AA, FM) sat at quarterly exam sessions. Completing both levels earns you the ACCA Advanced Diploma in Accounting and Business, before you move on to Strategic Professional.
