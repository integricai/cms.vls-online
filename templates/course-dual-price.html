ACCA SBR Exam Paper Overview

This course prepares students for the ACCA Strategic Business Reporting (SBR) exam with video lessons, study notes, question practice, quizzes, progress tests, tutor support and mock practice where included. Choose VLS for clear teaching, exam-focused practice and support from an experienced ACCA team.

ACCA SBR Exam Syllabus and Structure The SBR syllabus covers the financial reporting framework, group accounting, financial instruments, leases, provisions, deferred tax, foreign currency, performance reporting and current developments in corporate reporting. You will learn to apply IFRS standards to complex scenarios and develop the professional judgement the examiner expects. The ACCA SBR exam is a 4-hour computer-based case-study paper. Section A contains two compulsory questions built around a scenario; Section B offers a choice of two from three questions. Success depends on structured answers, effective time management and the ability to link standards to the facts in the case. To pass SBR first time, work through every module in order, attempt all question videos under timed conditions, and use the mock exam to identify gaps before your real attempt.

Questions, answered

Frequently asked questions.

Why do students most often fail SBR?
The most common reasons are weak consolidation technique, failing to apply standards to the specific scenario, neglecting ethics and current developments, and poor time management across the two sections. This course targets each of those directly with scenario-based practice and a timed mock.
What support is included with the course?
You get direct access to your tutor through a dedicated WhatsApp study group, weekly live classes, and full marking and feedback on your final mock exam — so help is always close when you're stuck.
How long do I have access?
Your enrolment gives you 365 days of full access to every lesson, note and quiz, plus all recorded live classes — enough to cover a full exam cycle and resit window if you need it.
Is the course aligned to the latest ACCA syllabus?
Yes. The course is built to the current SBR syllabus and updated each exam cycle, with current developments and recently issued standards reflected throughout.
What is the SBR exam format?
SBR is a 3-hour 15-minute written exam in two sections: Section A (two compulsory case-study questions, 50 marks) and Section B (two further questions, 50 marks). The course's exam-technique module prepares you for both.
