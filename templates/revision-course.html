About the paper

What is ACCA F2 (Management Accounting)?

ACCA F2 — now examined as Management Accounting (MA) — is one of the three Applied Knowledge exams and a core building block of the ACCA qualification. It introduces the costing, budgeting and performance techniques that finance professionals use to plan, control and support decision-making inside an organisation.

The syllabus moves from the nature and purpose of management information through data analysis, cost accounting techniques, budgeting and standard costing, and finishes with performance measurement. Because so much of F2 is calculation-based and time-pressured, it rewards structured practice far more than passive reading — which is exactly what a dedicated revision course is built to deliver. This Vertex F2 revision course assumes you have already studied the material once and now need to consolidate, practise and build exam speed. If you're starting from scratch, our full ACCA F2 Complete Learning Pack teaches every topic first — then this revision course sharpens you for the exam.

Full coverage

ACCA F2 syllabus areas covered

Your revision covers all six areas of the current ACCA Management Accounting (MA) syllabus, A to F.

A

Nature & purpose of management information

Management vs financial accounting, cost classification and behaviour, and information for planning, control and decision-making.

B

Data analysis & statistical techniques

Sampling, averages and spread, index numbers, correlation and regression, time series and forecasting.

C

Cost accounting techniques

Material, labour and overhead costs, absorption and marginal costing, job, batch, process and service costing, and ABC.

D

Budgeting

Budget preparation, functional and cash budgets, flexible budgets and budgetary control.

E

Standard costing

Setting standards, calculating materials, labour, overhead and sales variances, and reconciling budget to actual.

F

Performance measurement

Financial and non-financial performance, divisional performance measures and the balanced scorecard.

Questions, answered

ACCA F2 revision FAQs.

What is the ACCA F2 (Management Accounting) exam format?
F2 is a two-hour, computer-based exam worth 100 marks. Section A has 35 objective test questions worth 2 marks each (70 marks), and Section B has 3 multi-task questions worth 10 marks each (30 marks) covering budgeting, standard costing and performance measurement. All questions are compulsory, there's no negative marking, and the pass mark is 50%.
Do I need to have studied F2 before taking this revision course?
Ideally, yes. This course is designed to consolidate and sharpen knowledge you've already covered. If you're starting F2 from scratch, begin with our full ACCA F2 Complete Learning Pack, which teaches every topic first — then use this revision course to build exam speed before your sitting.
How long does it take to revise for F2?
Most students complete the revision comfortably in two to three weeks alongside other commitments. The course includes a suggested three-week plan, but with 365 days of access you can go faster or slower and revisit any topic before your exam.
How many practice questions and mock exams are included?
You get more than 700 exam-standard practice questions with fully worked solutions, plus three complete CBE-style mock exams that replicate the real two-hour format, and Section B multi-task question drills.
Is F2 hard to pass?
F2 is very passable with structured practice. It's calculation-heavy and time-pressured, so the students who struggle usually haven't done enough timed questions. This course is built around exactly that — recap quickly, then practise under exam conditions until the techniques are automatic.
Is the course aligned to the latest ACCA MA syllabus?
Yes. The course covers all six syllabus areas (A–F) of the current ACCA Management Accounting (MA) syllabus and is reviewed each exam cycle so the content, question style and mock exams stay in line with what ACCA examines.
